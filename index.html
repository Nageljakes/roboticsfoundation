Parent Coaching Guide 💡

This game uses the exact cognitive scaffolding recommended by early STEM educators to prepare a 5-year-old for robotics:

  • Hardware Components: Robots aren't magic. In the Workshop, discuss how computers need inputs (camera/sensors) and outputs (wheels/grippers).
  • Sequential Logic: Computers can only execute instructions in exact chronological order (First step 1, then step 2).
  • Relative Frame of Reference: Turning "Left" or "Right" is from the Robot's perspective, not the child's perspective. If she turns the wrong way, ask: "Which hand is the robot's right hand?"
  • Debugging: In robotics, errors are not failures! If the bot hits a rock or misses, say: "You found a bug! Let's debug the sequence together!"
