Parent Coaching Guide 💡

Everything here is built for a 5-year-old who is just starting out with robotics. Nothing needs reading, since the robot speaks every instruction.

  • Hardware: In the Workshop, talk about inputs (eyes and sensors) and outputs (wheels and hands). Robots aren't magic, they are parts that work together.
  • Sequencing: Computers do step 1, then step 2, in exact order. Tap a step on the program tape to remove it.
  • Left and right: Turns are from the robot's point of view. The yellow arrow shows where it faces. Ask: "Which is the robot's right hand?"
  • Debugging: If the robot bumps a rock, say "You found a bug! Let's debug it together." The 💡 Help button points at the next step to try.
  • Stars: 3 stars = the shortest program. Replaying for fewer steps teaches efficiency.
  • Privacy: Works fully offline. Progress is saved only on this device, with no accounts and no tracking.
